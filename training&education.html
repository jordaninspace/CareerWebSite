Training and Education Required to be a Cyber Security Engineer

Many employers prefer a bachelors degree in computer Science, cybersecurity, information technology, or computer engineering. However, A degree is not always a strict prerequisite; data shows many professionals enter the field with associate degrees, bootcamps, or intensive self-taught training combined with practical proof of skills and cirtificates such as CompTIAsecurity+. For advanced or leadership positions some companies may require a masters degress in cyber security or infomation assurance which often take 12-18 months to complete.
